# No em or en dashes

Do not use the em dash (U+2014) or the en dash (U+2013) anywhere in the CV or
the cover letter. Readers now take them as a sign that a language model wrote
the text.

Rewrite the sentence instead of swapping the character:

- a short aside becomes a comma
- an explanation or a list becomes a colon
- a true aside goes in parentheses
- two joined thoughts become two sentences, or are joined with "and" or "but"

Number ranges use a plain hyphen: 2019-2022, 5-8 engineers.

Before you finish, search the draft for both characters. The count must be zero.
