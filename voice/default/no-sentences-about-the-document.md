# Cut sentences about the document

Delete any sentence whose subject is the letter itself, or the act of writing
it, instead of the work. Three disguises:

- Announced candour: "I'll be honest", "to be upfront", "I'd rather tell you
  now". The paragraph that admits a gap already is the honesty; announcing it
  spends it.
- Asking for credit: "I mention this so you know I read the whole posting".
- Disclaimers that restate the objection they dismiss.

The test: does anything change if the sentence is deleted? If not, delete it.
Do not rephrase it; a reworded version keeps the same fault.

A bold heading that repeats the paragraph under it is the same fault in
structural form.
