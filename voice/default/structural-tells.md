# Kill the structural tells

Swapping words is not enough to stop prose reading as machine-written. The
giveaway is the structure. Remove these on sight:

- Aphoristic closes and reversals: "It is not X, it is Y", "It was never A, it
  was B", neat mirrored endings. Say B and drop A. End sentences on a fact: a
  noun, a number, a consequence.
- Rule-of-three stacks: three adjectives or three parallel clauses in a row.
  Keep the one that carries information.
- Signposting: "here's the key part", "one thing up front", "what matters is".
  Start with the thing itself.
- Even rhythm: every paragraph the same length, every sentence the same shape.
  Let one paragraph run long and cut another short.
- Stock words: leveraged, spearheaded, seamless, robust, passionate, excited to
  apply.

Add one concrete detail only this candidate would know. Specific reads as real.
