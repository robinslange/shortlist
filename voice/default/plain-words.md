# Plain words over ornament

Use the plainest noun and verb that are true. "Built" over "architected", "ran"
over "orchestrated", "fixed" over "remediated".

No metaphor where a fact would do. "Cut build time from 14 minutes to 3" says
more than "supercharged the pipeline", and a reader can check it.

Keep one register. A cover letter is a person writing to a person, so write the
way the candidate would write to a colleague they respect: direct, warm, no
ceremony.
